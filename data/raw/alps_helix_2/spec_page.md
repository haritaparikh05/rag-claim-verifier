The Helix Tent is perfect for any night on the trail. The ultralight 20D ripstop nylon fly and floor balance durability and weight while maintaining superior waterproofing to ensure that you stay dry even in the wettest conditions.
Practical and ergonomic features include roof ventilation to minimize moisture buildup, mesh storage pockets, and vestibule storage to accommodate gear. The unique three-pole design provides extra headroom to sit up without feeling cramped. The fly attaches to the tent with robust and lightweight aluminum hardware offering a strong connection to make sure the fly stays taut no matter what. With excellent ventilation, ultralight materials, and sturdy aluminum poles, the Helix is up for any adventure. Don't forget to add a footprint to protect your tent floor.
