Description
Our iconic 32oz Wide Mouth water bottles are perfect for everyday use, outdoor sports like hiking, camping, and climbing, and more. The wide opening fits most filtration devices and ice cubes, and the design is durable and versatile. Thoughtfully made from Tritan Renew. BPA/BPS-Free. Made in the USA. Leakproof and Dishwasher-Safe.
Features
BPA/BPS Free
So you can drink water that's safe and tastes great.

Fits most filtration units for clean water in a pinch.
Fits Ice Cubes
Put ice in your bottle with ease and enjoy your frosty beverage anywhere.
Leak-Proof Guarantee

Lightweight
